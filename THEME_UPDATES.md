# Vyomaa Energy CRM - Theme Updates

## 🎨 Brand Theme Implementation

The application has been updated with Vyomaa Energy's brand colors and modern design aesthetics.

### Color Palette Applied

#### Primary Colors
- **Primary Green**: `#56D54F` - Main brand color
- **Green Hover**: `#3AA835` - Hover states and darker accents
- **Light Green**: `#8DE778` - Light accents

#### Accent Colors
- **Lime Accent**: `#BAFF00` - Success states and highlights
- **Lime Light**: `#D9FF66` - Subtle accents

#### Orange/Warning Colors
- **Orange Primary**: `#FC702F` - Warnings and important metrics
- **Orange Light**: `#FF9A63` - Light warnings
- **Orange Dark**: `#D85A1B` - Dark warnings

#### Neutral Colors
- **Dark**: `#1C242E` - Navigation, dark backgrounds
- **Light Gray**: `#F5F5F5` - Backgrounds
- **White**: `#FEFEFE` - Paper, cards
